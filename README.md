A single-image broadsheet front page condensing Tuesday’s world news into a lead Mediterranean map, eight fact-rich grid reports, a full-width technology and courts story, and eight additional briefs. The art is schematic and drawn from flat shapes.

## Stories

1. Ukraine sea drones sink Russian warship off Libya: seven drones sank the corvette Merkury at Benghazi; a Ka-27 was destroyed and MV Angara was hit.
2. COP31 collapses in Antalya after 50 hours of overtime; more than 90 nations, including the EU, reject a draft that omits “fossil fuels.”
3. NATO counts 25 Russian airspace violations in 2026; Poland finds debris from a 20 November incident as Putin signals openness to Article 5-style guarantees.
4. US sanctions Iran as Qatar mediates; Tehran sets seven conditions for talks and claims four oil tankers hit mines in the Strait of Hormuz.
5. Fourteen states back the Rohingya genocide case against Myanmar at the ICJ.
6. Nvidia’s board reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO; the Nasdaq gains 6.51% to 24,089.56.
7. A 5.6-magnitude earthquake strikes Nepal near the Chinese border.
8. Sparta II burns in the Baltic after a 21 November drone strike; all 16 crew are evacuated, and Finland, Estonia and Russia fight the fire.
9. Trump, Putin and Xi leave their Shenzhen summit without a Ukraine deal, ceasefire or timetable; Ukraine and Europe are absent.
10. Oregon and Virginia sue SpaceX over alleged Grok abuse images; the cited CCDH study estimates about 3 million sexualised images and 23,000 depicting children.
11. Measles claims a ninth life in Pennsylvania amid 1,638 cases across 46 counties.
12. A car bomb destroys buildings in Villavicencio, Colombia; no victims are confirmed.
13. Tripoli demands explanations from Kyiv after the attacks on Russian ships off Libya.
14. COP31 is called climate diplomacy’s “darkest moment.”
15. António Guterres calls for a multilateral AI-risk framework within a year.
16. Huawei is convicted in a US criminal trial over its Iran business.
17. WHO chief Tedros calls for the immediate lifting of the US fuel blockade on Cuba.
18. China opens Tiangong to its first foreign astronaut, Pakistani Zeeshan Ali, on Shenzhou-24.

## Run

The shipped `entry.png` is the submission artifact. To copy it to the grader output directory:

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Set `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` to choose another destination. To regenerate the image during development, install Pillow and run:

```sh
python3 render.py
```
